# Ari v0.3.14

Ari 0.3.14 brings a calmer interface, lets you run agents on other computers you own, and gives Aria an optional voice that tells you what she is doing while she works. New sessions now start with full permissions by default.

## A quieter interface

- The app is now a quiet frame around one stage. Hover and selection are soft washes, primary buttons are plain ink, and the accent colour is kept for what needs you.
- Empty sessions, the welcome screen and onboarding show your wallpaper as dithered art behind a centred composer.
- Session cards in the sidebar put status, host, project, agent, branch and age on one line above the title.
- The composer rests as a single row. Model, reasoning and permission are compact text menus, one line per choice.
- Settings opens as a window over your work, on a dimmed, blurred background, instead of replacing the screen.
- Every scroll area shares one scrollbar style.
- Files open in an inline editor, and the explorer no longer reloads in a loop.
- Charts in replies use one colour-blind-safe palette. Near-miss chart data from agents is repaired instead of failing; long or numerous bars become ranked rows.

## Machines

- Pair other computers you own and run sessions on them from this one. Projects and sessions show which machine they live on.
- New sessions can choose a machine, checkout and workspace. Features a remote machine can't offer are explained in the conversation.
- Files on a remote machine open read-only when they can't be saved.
- Sessions in separate worktrees of the same project can run at the same time.
- Tailscale Serve keeps mappings Ari didn't create and recovers Ari's own addresses after a restart.

## Aria

- Aria is now an experiment, off by default. Turn her on in Settings → Experimental.
- She can speak her replies with a local Kokoro voice, or with Groq if you add a key. She speaks each step as she reaches it ("Finding the sessions to delete") instead of one summary at the end.
- Ordinary words missing from the voice dictionary are no longer spelled out letter by letter.
- General dictation has a neutral indicator, separate from Aria's.

## Speech input

- Finished dictation is formatted on your computer, and your draft text is kept. Text held back for review appears in a small popover.

## Reliability

- Stall watchdogs only watch active agent work, so idle Aria and agent sessions are no longer marked stalled.

## Upgrade notes

Install over 0.3.13. Saved default permissions of Ask or Edits, in Settings and in each project's saved agent, change once to Full auto. If you choose Ask again later, Ari keeps it. Existing sessions keep their current mode.

Aria is off after upgrading. Turn her on in Settings → Experimental. The Kokoro voice is an optional download and uses about 150 MB more memory while loaded.

## Known gaps

- Installers are unsigned.
- Machines were checked against test fixtures, not across real paired computers. Live voice turns and the mobile app were not checked by hand.
